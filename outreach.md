I participate in K-12 science outreach in the Houston area, primarily
through the University of Houston Department of Physics. I enjoy showing
kids the parts of physics that are surprising before they are explained,
and the parts that are tactile enough to stay with you.

## Recent activities

**May 2026: Physics demos at the Martin Luther King Jr. Early Childhood
Center, Houston.** Volunteered with Prof. Lisa Koerner's UH physics group
for a morning of hands-on demos for approximately 150 pre-K and
elementary students across five rotating groups. Stations included a
rotating-stool demonstration of angular momentum conservation, an
electromagnetism demo, and hands-on play with spinning tops, magnets,
and simple gyroscopes.
